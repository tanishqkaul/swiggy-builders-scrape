Your Menstrual Cycle Changes Everything

Progesterone, estrogen, and prostaglandins fluctuate throughout your cycle. These hormones directly control appetite, food preference, and craving intensity. CravingsAPI predicts what you'll want based on where you are in your cycle.

🩸
Menstrual
Days 1–5
Warm comfort food, iron-rich meals, chocolate. Prostaglandins crave easy-to-digest warmth.
🌱
Follicular
Days 6–13
Light, fresh meals. Rising estrogen suppresses appetite and increases energy.

Social eating, restaurants, protein. Peak energy and social appetite.
